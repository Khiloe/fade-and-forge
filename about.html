Our Story

Built on craft, not shortcuts

Fade & Forge started with one chair and a simple belief: a haircut should feel worth the wait.

Since 2013

A shop that respects your time

Fade & Forge opened its doors in Braamfontein over a decade ago with a clear goal: to bring back the kind of barbering where the barber actually knows your name, your head shape and exactly how you like your fade.

We're not a chain. We're not a production line. Every barber here has earned their chair — and every client gets the same care whether it's their first visit or their hundredth.

The name says it all. A fade is precision and technique. A forge is where things are shaped with heat, patience and skill. That's how we approach every cut.

What we stand for

Our principles

Consistency

The same great cut, every single time. We don't do "off days" behind the chair.

Respect

For your time, your money and your look. We listen before we pick up the clippers.

Community

We're a Braamfontein shop, through and through. We sponsor local sport and hire locally.
